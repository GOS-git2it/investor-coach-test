Get started

1. Tap the big button.

Open my FL Exam Coach

2. Enter your code:

MAGIC-123

3. Tap Open my coach.

A short tour shows you around. To see it again, tap How it works at the top right of Home.

Tip: add it to your Home Screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Every day (1 to 2 hours)

  1. Tap your course, then Get coached, then Start.
  2. Tap the Lessons tab. Tap today's lesson and listen.
  3. Tap I finished listening, then Ready.
  4. Answer 10 questions with A B C D. Tap Next question after each one.
  5. Score 80% or higher? Your coach says Lesson passed. Tap the button to open the next lesson.
  6. Done for the day? Tap 🏠 Home. Your session saves by itself.

Stuck on a question?

Type in the Ask your coach box. You get a hint, not the answer. Tap the 💡 to read your hints again. Tap Got it to go back.

Good to know

  • Lessons open one at a time. Yellow means open. Locked lessons open when you pass the one before.
  • You can replay any lesson you already passed.
  • Leave a quiz halfway or step away 30 minutes? It starts over from question 1.
  • Your coach only helps with Florida insurance and this exam. It will not promise a pass.

Ready to book?

After you pass all 12 lessons, a Practice exam button opens on your course menu. It has 50 questions that cover all 12 lessons. There are no hints.

Score 80% or higher (40 of 50). The page then lists the lessons to re-listen to.

Also score 80% on a Pearson practice test. Do not book your retake before that. GiO will tell you.

Quick fixes

  • Coach is busy: wait a few seconds and tap again.
  • No audio: check silent mode. Close the page and open it again.
  • Asks for your code: enter MAGIC-123 again.
  • Page looks old: close it and reopen from your Home Screen icon.
